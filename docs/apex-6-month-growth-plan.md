# Apex Tune Hub 6-Month Growth Plan

Goal: turn Apex Tune Hub from an FH6 tuning MVP into a durable racing-game tool site. Revenue should start with search traffic, AdSense, affiliate links, email, and later lightweight membership features.

## Month 1: Trust, Indexing, Core Product

Core goal: make Google and players understand that Apex Tune Hub is an FH6 tuning tool site, not a generic article blog.

- Keep the homepage focused on the Tune Calculator and problem-led entry paths.
- Keep `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, and IndexNow healthy.
- Review GSC weekly: indexing, impressions, clicks, CTR, and crawl issues.
- Prioritize these URLs:
  - `/`
  - `/games/forza-horizon-6`
  - `/games/forza-horizon-6/guides`
  - `/games/forza-horizon-6/cars`
  - `/games/forza-horizon-6/weekly-playlist`
  - `/tools/forza-horizon-6-tune-calculator`
  - `/tools/forza-horizon-6-drift-tune-calculator`
  - `/tools/forza-horizon-6-gear-ratio-calculator`
  - high-intent handling guides
  - best-car pages
- KPI:
  - 20+ indexed pages
  - daily GSC impressions
  - tool pages accessible, fast, and free of major UX problems

## Month 2: High-Intent Long-Tail Content

Core goal: build the first batch of pages that can rank and route users into the calculator workflow.

- Publish or expand 30-50 useful FH6 pages.
- Page types:
  - drift setup
  - road racing setup
  - rally setup
  - gear ratio tuning
  - wheel settings
  - controller settings
  - Steam Deck settings
  - starter cars
  - weekly playlist notes
- Every page should include:
  - a useful table
  - FAQ
  - internal links
  - a calculator handoff
  - source notes where relevant
- Avoid pure AI article pages. The moat is data, tools, workflows, screenshots, and update discipline.
- KPI:
  - GSC impressions grow weekly
  - at least 10 pages reach top 50 average position for some query

## Month 3: Car Database Moat

Core goal: make car pages and car-category pages support the tools.

- Expand toward 100-200 car pages.
- Each car page should include:
  - best race type
  - class fit
  - drivetrain fit
  - road/drift/drag/dirt baseline notes
  - strengths and weaknesses
  - common tuning problems
  - matching calculator preset links
- Build and strengthen collection pages:
  - JDM cars
  - starter cars
  - best drift cars
  - best road racing cars
  - rally cars
  - S1/S2/A/B class cars
- KPI:
  - car pages start bringing long-tail clicks
  - tool pages receive more internal traffic from guides and car pages

## Month 4: Monetization Tests

Core goal: validate which traffic has commercial value.

- Apply for AdSense only after the site has enough useful indexed pages.
- Keep ads low-noise so calculator UX stays clean.
- Test affiliate directions:
  - Xbox Game Pass
  - controllers
  - racing wheels
  - racing seats
  - Steam Deck / PC accessories
- Build 3-5 buyer-intent pages:
  - best wheel for FH6
  - best controller settings and controller picks
  - Steam Deck FH6 guide
  - budget racing setup
  - wheel stand / cockpit setup
- Start weekly email updates once subscription capture is working.
- KPI:
  - first ad or affiliate revenue
  - weekly email list growth

## Month 5: Advanced Tools and Community Signals

Core goal: make the site useful enough for repeat visits.

- Improve saved presets and shareable calculator links.
- Add weekly playlist tune packs.
- Prepare user-submitted tune code or setup-note workflows.
- Shape future member features:
  - favorite cars
  - saved presets across devices
  - exportable setup notes
  - weekly tune packs
  - comparison tables
- KPI:
  - returning visitors increase
  - tool pages have higher engagement than article-only pages
  - save/copy/share events appear regularly

## Month 6: Scale Winners

Core goal: use data from months 1-5 to focus on pages that can become stable traffic and revenue.

- Use GSC to find high-impression, low-CTR pages and rewrite:
  - title
  - meta description
  - H1
  - first screen answer
- Expand pages with clicks and tool events.
- Prune or merge thin pages with no impressions, no links, and no tool path.
- Turn winning pages into clusters:
  - best S1 cars
  - best drift cars
  - weekly playlist tune pack
  - wheel settings
  - gear ratio troubleshooting
- Test a lightweight membership only after clear repeat-use signals exist.
- KPI:
  - stable organic traffic
  - at least two revenue channels active
  - first paying or high-intent repeat users identified

## Weekly Operating Routine

- Monday: review GSC and Vercel Analytics.
- Tuesday: update 3-5 existing pages with FAQ, tables, and internal links.
- Wednesday: publish 2-3 high-intent pages if data supports them.
- Thursday: expand car data or preset data.
- Friday: improve tool UX and conversion paths.
- Weekend: prepare weekly playlist, newsletter, or source-note updates.

Use these two detailed playbooks:

- `docs/apex-fh6-analytics-playbook.md`
- `docs/apex-fh6-content-production-playbook.md`

## Revenue Priority

1. AdSense: realistic once useful pages and search traffic exist.
2. Affiliate: wheels, controllers, Game Pass, Steam Deck, PC accessories.
3. Newsletter sponsorship: only after a meaningful list exists.
4. Membership: only after save/share/export workflows prove repeat value.

## Risk Control

- Do not use official Forza logos, key art, or trademarks as Apex Tune Hub brand assets.
- Keep the brand as Apex Tune Hub, not an official Forza property.
- Keep independent/not-affiliated language visible where appropriate.
- Use official sources for release, platform, and edition claims.
- Label untested setup logic as baseline, launch-prep, or expected workflow.
- Do not copy creator videos, thumbnails, transcripts, or article text.
